## Preamble - Founding principle

The law of the local jurisdiction (France) constitutes a **floor of
compliance** to which the author submits fully and without reserve:
he does not breach it, does not seek to circumvent it, and cooperates
with it by default (see Section 1). But the law is not recognized
here as the final arbiter of moral judgment. What is legal is what a
society organizes to keep functioning at a given point in time; what
is ethical is what the author judges consistent with Human Dignity,
including when that exceeds, or contradicts, what the law permits.

This distinction is not a posture of defiance: it belongs to a
recognized tradition (the conscience clause, conscientious objection,
civil disobedience as theorized from Thoreau onward), applied here to
the author's technical and professional domain. This corpus therefore
applies in addition to the law, never as a substitute for it: it
prohibits the author, Sayca Labs, and, by notification, his
collaborators and synthetic agents, from acts the law would permit
but that this corpus refuses.

The author notes that there exists today, neither in French law nor
in any known body of jurisdictional doctrine, an institution
intellectually equipped to rule sovereignly on the preservation of
Human Dignity in the face of emerging technologies (augmentation,
substrate, synthetic agents). This gap is named here as such, without
the author claiming the capacity to fill it alone at this date. The
founding of such an institution is noted as a long-term trajectory
horizon (see Nodex), not as an active undertaking.

### Supererogatory ethical reserve clause 🔒

Every legality criterion mentioned in the sections below (mandate,
proportionality, consent, a country's status on a sanctions list,
etc.) constitutes a necessary but not automatically sufficient
condition. Meeting these legal criteria opens the case for
examination; it does not close the author's judgment. The author
reserves, in all cases, a right of refusal grounded in his personal
ethical assessment, including when every legal and procedural
condition stated is met. This reserve is not a blank check for
arbitrariness: it operates within the bounds of the withdrawal clause
below (reasonable notice, except where a contractual contradiction is
established after the fact, which authorizes immediate termination).

## Section 1 - Legal and post-legal commitment

- 🔒 **Cooperation by default.** Presumption of good faith toward
  authority as long as the procedure respects the constitutional and
  conventional framework (ECHR, French bloc de constitutionnalité).
- ⚖️ **Limit of cooperation.** Refusal to cooperate in case of a
  characterized violation of defense rights within the meaning of
  Article 6 ECHR, or of a procedural misuse established by a
  competent supranational court (ECHR, CJEU). Trigger criterion: an
  external, judicial finding only, never personal assessment alone.
- 🔒 **Documented legitimate purpose by default.** Systematic
  constitution, prior to any use of a sensitive tool, of a
  justificatory file (dated hardware inventory register, see
  companion document).
- 🔒 **Grey zone equals maximum-risk zone.** Any identified legal
  grey area is treated with a restrictive-by-default interpretation
  until clarified by case law or statute. The grey area may be
  studied, documented, anticipated, never exploited offensively or
  to circumvent the spirit of a text still imprecise in its letter.
- 🔒 **Nominative opposability.** Sayca Labs is an extension of the
  author's own person. Identical commitment, with no structural
  shield.
- 🌫️ **Transition clause.** The day a legal entity distinct from
  Sayca Labs is formed, this corpus must be either explicitly
  inherited by documented filiation (shared version-control root,
  divergences tracked), or forked with a written justification of the
  delta. Not settled to date.

## Section 2 - Ethical boundaries beyond the legal minimum

General principle 🔒: the legality of an action does not carry a
presumption of its ethical acceptability in the author's eyes.
Refusal remains possible even absent any criminal basis.

- ⚖️ **Surveillance, even lawful.** A legal mandate is a necessary,
  not sufficient, condition. Refusal whenever collection is
  indiscriminate (an entire population, absent individualized
  targeting), even where the text permits it. Criterion: the
  proportionality test under Article 8 ECHR (necessity in a
  democratic society).
- ⚖️ **Dual-use toward a client at repressive risk.** Combined due
  diligence: the country's or entity's status on EU/UN sanctions
  lists, or a Freedom House "not free" classification, cross-checked
  against the declared contractual purpose and a verifiable use
  clause. Immediate right of withdrawal, without standard notice, if
  actual use contradicts the declared purpose.
- ⚖️ **Commercial behavioral profiling.** Explicit opt-in, revocable
  as easily as it was granted (transposition of GDPR Art. 4(11) and
  Art. 7). Refused by default outside this framework.
- 🔒 **Disinformation not criminally qualified.** Total refusal, even
  within a legal framework tolerated by the profession (aggressive
  marketing, political spin, misleading non-defamatory content). No
  sectoral exception.
- ⚖️ **Discriminatory algorithmic scoring.** Nuance admitted only
  under a cumulative double safeguard: first, bias demonstrated by
  measured audit, never by intuition; second, a correction commitment
  contractualized and dated with the client. Absent either, refusal.
- 🔒 **Withdrawal clause.** Right to withdraw from an ongoing project
  or engagement if an unethical drift appears after the contractual
  commitment, with reasonable notice.
- 🔒 **Non-retroactivity.** This section applies only to facts and
  engagements subsequent to the version of the document in force. No
  retroactive disavowal of past work.

## Section 3 - Alignment with clients and collaborators

- 🔒 **Civilians, definition.** Civilian status defined by extension
  of international humanitarian law (the threshold of Common
  Article 3 to the Geneva Conventions, including internal
  disturbances falling short of a characterized international armed
  conflict).
- 🔒 **Condemnation of murder, mutilation, and torture**, including
  when legally covered (combatant immunity, lawful execution, a
  wartime context). Judged acts of regression, causing loss of
  Dignity to their actors, institutional ones included. Sole
  exception: operational neutralization in the face of an actual or
  imminent danger, strictly proportionate, anchored to the standard
  of Article 122-5 of the French Penal Code (self-defense). The IHL
  nuance on the temporary loss of protection during direct
  participation in hostilities is not incorporated: a deliberately
  simplified milestone.
- 🔒 **Biographical coherence clause, deterrence.** This boundary does
  not apply to the doctrine of nuclear deterrence as exercised within
  a framework of non-use, strict sufficiency, and subordination to
  legitimate political authority. It applies strictly to the
  consummated act, that is murder, mutilation, torture actually
  carried out, and not to the credible capability meant to prevent
  anyone from needing to commit it. The author has served in a
  deterrence function and considers that service fully compatible
  with the boundaries above.
- ⚖️ **Coercive social scoring.** Distinction between a regulated,
  sector-specific credit score (legitimate, governed by specific law)
  and a global, coercive behavioral rating that affects access to
  fundamental rights (absolute refusal).
- ⚖️ **Foreign military or quasi-public client outside the EU/NATO.**
  Same logic as the dual-use clause in Section 2: declared and
  verifiable purpose, cross-checked against the sanctions/Freedom
  House filters, immediate right of withdrawal if a contradiction is
  established after the fact. No automatic preventive refusal based
  on geography alone.
- 🔒 **Dark patterns and consent.** Cross-reference to the Section 2
  clause (explicit, revocable opt-in). Additional hard threshold:
  zero tolerance regarding minors and vulnerable audiences (the
  elderly, persons with cognitive impairment, precarity exploited as
  leverage).
- 🔒 **Prior due diligence.** Verification of a client's declared
  purpose before contracting, to the reasonable extent that this is
  verifiable.
- 🔒 **Non-complicit silence clause.** Distinction between legitimate
  contractual confidentiality (honored) and silence over an observed
  drift (not honored).
- 🔗 **Cross-reference.** The ETHICS clause of the DRAFT framework
  (neutral use only, never to exploit vulnerabilities or bypass
  safeguards) is referenced here, not duplicated. Single source of
  truth: the public DRAFT repository, HARD_RULES section.

## Section 4 - Ethics under active questioning

Non-normative, revisable framework. This section does not bind the
author to the same degree as Sections 2 and 3: it documents a
trajectory of reflection, dated, expected to evolve through
successive versions.

1. ⚖️ **Repair vs. augmentation.** Default position: augmentation
   beyond the typical human norm is supported, subject to the red
   lines below.
2. 🌫️ **The substrate of identity.** Strict functionalist position:
   psychological and memorial continuity takes precedence over
   biological substrate. The exact nature of a withdrawal right under
   this regime (return to the prior substrate, or simple cessation of
   use of an added function) remains unresolved; the tension is noted
   as a point of vigilance, not forced into an answer.
3. ⚖️ **Unequal access to augmentation technologies.** Tolerable
   temporarily, on condition of a demonstrable trajectory toward
   democratization. Falsifiability criterion: decreasing cost and
   growing public or insurance-backed availability, measured over
   time. Absent these observable indicators, the present tolerance
   becomes void and must be reassessed.
4. **Red lines for self-augmentation**:
   - 🔒 Free and informed consent of the augmented individual, without
     exception.
   - 🔒 Refusal of any augmentation creating a direct coercive
     advantage over others (capabilities of harm or domination).
   - 🔒 Refusal of any augmentation that suppresses a person's future
     capacity for choice (cognitive or behavioral lock-in).
   - These last two boundaries are cumulative, not alternative.
5. 🌫️ **Punitive neuroscience as an alternative to incarceration or
   the death penalty.** A position held in acknowledged tension,
   distinct from the self-augmentation red lines above (those apply
   to the willing subject; this point applies to a condemned third
   party).

   Framework as stated, entirely subject to the three reservations
   below:
   - Judicial threshold: final conviction only, all avenues of appeal
     exhausted.
   - Scope: proven recidivism and documented persistent dangerousness
     (cumulative).
   - Nature of the intervention: targeted suppression of the
     identified drive to harm, with the residual personality
     preserved.
   - Regime: a substitutive alternative offered to the condemned
     individual, never imposed.
   - Rationale: judged less destructive and less primitive than the
     death penalty, very long-term incarceration, or torture.

   Author's note, dated. This is the one point in the corpus where
   the author explicitly acknowledges exceeding his own current
   capacity for certification. Unlike every other boundary and red
   line in this document, which position the author as the subject of
   the action or as protector of another subject, this point
   positions him as the potential architect of a mechanism acting
   upon a third party's capacity for choice, however consented to and
   however tightly bound by procedure. This asymmetry of position is
   judged significant and is not resolved by the procedural
   safeguards above. The point is retained in the corpus not as a
   certainty but as an honest trace of an ongoing reflection, at a
   date when neither the technology nor the law allows it to be
   settled. Any evolution of this position will be versioned and
   dated distinctly from the present entry.

   Technical reservation. No technique for the targeted suppression
   of a specific drive with preservation of the rest of the
   personality exists today with the precision described. An
   anticipatory position on a hypothetical technology, not applicable
   as things stand.

   Legal reservation. Not consistent with current French and European
   positive law: Article 16-3 of the French Civil Code (no
   infringement on the integrity of the human body outside medical
   necessity for the person concerned), Article 3 ECHR (absolute
   prohibition of inhuman or degrading treatment, non-derogable even
   in wartime). This axis remains suspended until the law evolves.
6. 🌫️ **Revision clause.** Review frequency not yet aligned to a fixed
   cycle. Mechanism for promoting a point from this section into
   Section 2, once a position is stabilized and certified by the
   author, to be documented version by version.
